We do not use the data for profiling, automated decision-making, advertising, or analytics, and we do not sell or share it. Do not store special categories of personal data (Art. 9) in notebooks. Data is processed on University infrastructure in Italy.

Public notebook gallery

The gallery at /services/demos/ needs no account. Notebooks execute in your browser (WebAssembly); edits and uploads stay in your browser's local storage and are never sent to our servers. To run Python, your browser downloads the Pyodide runtime from the jsDelivr content-delivery network (and, for packages not bundled with the gallery, from the Python Package Index), which therefore receives your IP address; notebooks also request public forecasts from api.meteo.uniparthenope.it.

Cookies and local storage

Only strictly necessary cookies are used, so no consent banner is required (Directive 2002/58/EC, Art. 5(3)):

  • jupyterhub-hub-login, jupyterhub-session-id, and jupyterhub-user-…: keep you signed in and route you to your server; at most {{ wxhub_cookie_days }} days or until you sign out.
  • _xsrf: protects forms against cross-site request forgery; session.
  • Browser local storage remembers your day/night theme and that you have read the cookie notice.

Your rights

You have the rights of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), portability (Art. 20), and objection (Art. 21). Signed-in users can view and download their data and submit requests on the My data page; workspace files can be downloaded at any time from JupyterLab. You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian supervisory authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

Security

Each user server runs in an isolated container without elevated privileges; shared model and sensor archives are mounted read-only; administrative access is limited to designated staff and is recorded in an audit log.
